Our Story

Bridging Maharashtra's Produce with Global Markets

Flowex GlobalTrade LLP is an ambitious, emerging trade coordination and import-export enterprise based in Maharashtra, India. We facilitate the sourcing and supply of quality Indian products—including agricultural goods, food products, spices, textiles, and general merchandise—to global opportunities.

Committed to Quality

Our Mission

To operate with professionalism and transparency in every transaction. As a growing trade partner, we strive to coordinate the export and import of Indian goods efficiently, securely, and ethically, helping businesses navigate trade requirements with ease.

Our Vision

To establish a reliable, vetted network of suppliers and buyers that fosters mutual growth. We envision Flowex GlobalTrade as a trusted coordination partner, known for transparent operations and professional trade facilitation.

Core Principles

The Values That Drive Us

Quality Assurance

Sourcing from verified suppliers and coordinating quality checks to ensure shipments match buyer requirements.

Ethical Operations

Transparency is our baseline. We believe in fair practices, clear communication, and building long-term trust rather than chasing short-term gains.

Sustainable Growth

As an emerging trade company, we are dedicated to continuous learning, adapting to market demands, and scaling our coordination operations responsibly.

Verified Registration

Registrations & Trade Credentials

Flowex Global Trade LLP operates strictly under verified trade registrations and coordinates cargo clearances matching formal regulatory bodies:

DGFT Registered

Registered under the Directorate General of Foreign Trade, Government of India, holding a valid Import Export Code (IEC).

MSME Enterprise

Registered as a micro, small, and medium enterprise under the Ministry of MSME, Govt. of India.

APEDA & Agricultural Export Requirements

We coordinate agricultural product sourcing and packaging in accordance with applicable export requirements and relevant APEDA guidelines.
