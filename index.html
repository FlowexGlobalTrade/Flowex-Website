The Global Export Process

Transparency and reliability at every step. From your initial inquiry to final delivery, we ensure a seamless logistics operation adhering to international trade standards.

Step 01

01. Inquiry

Submit your requirements. Our trade experts analyze your needs, specifications, and volume to prepare a tailored source.

Step 02

02. Product Selection

We source and finalize the exact products meeting your quality criteria from our extensive, vetted global network.

Step 03

03. Quotation

A comprehensive, transparent Proforma Invoice is issued, detailing unit costs, shipping terms (Incoterms), and estimated timelines.

Proforma Generation

FOB / CIF / CFR Terms Applied

Step 04

04. Sample Approval

Physical samples or detailed digital proofs are provided to ensure the product perfectly aligns with your expectations before bulk production.

Step 05

05. Inspection

Rigorous quality control protocols are executed. Third-party inspections can be arranged to guarantee compliance with international standards.

  • Visual Quality Check
  • Quantity Verification
  • Compliance Testing
Step 06

06. Packaging

Export-grade packaging ensures cargo integrity during transit. We utilize standard or custom materials based on the shipping mode.

Step 07

07. Documentation

Preparation of flawless shipping documents including Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Certificate of Origin.

Customs Clearance Ready

100% Compliance Assured

Step 08

08. Loading

Careful stuffing of containers under strict supervision to maximize space and prevent transit damage.

Step 09

09. Shipping

Vessel departs. We provide tracking information and regular status updates so you have full visibility of your cargo's journey.

Live Tracking
Step 10

10. Delivery

Arrival at destination port. Following customs clearance, the cargo is handed over, completing a successful and secure transaction.
